## SEGMENT 1: THE TWO DISEASES (0:30-2:00)

[CONCEPT]
There are two types of knowledge concentration. Both require extraction, but the methods differ.

**Disease 1: Tribal Knowledge**
The classic problem. Your expert has domain knowledge others lack—processes, shortcuts, relationships, intuition built over years. When people ask "how do we do X?"—everyone points to one person.

**Disease 2: The AI Gap**
This is new. Some employees are AI-forward and 3x more effective, while others work traditionally. The gap isn't knowledge about your business—it's knowledge about HOW to use AI tools effectively.

[VISUAL_CUE: Two-column comparison of Tribal Knowledge vs AI Gap]

[DOPAMINE_TRIGGER]
Here's the test: Do projects stall when someone is on vacation? Are new hires dependent on shadowing one "expert" for months? If yes—you have knowledge trapped.

**The framework is simple: Extract → Package → Distribute.**

Think of it like cooking. You watch grandma make the recipe (Extract). You write it down with measurements (Package). You share the cookbook (Distribute). Without all three steps, the knowledge stays in one head.

## SEGMENT 2: EXTRACTION METHODS (2:00-3:30)

[EXAMPLE]
There are THREE ways to extract expert knowledge:

### Method 1: Use Existing Recordings
Your organization has more extraction material than you realize.

Meeting recordings, training videos, Loom walkthroughs—these contain expert knowledge already captured. A transcript from a 1-hour training can become a Standard Operating Procedure in MINUTES with AI.

[PATTERN_INTERRUPT]
[VISUAL_CUE: Show workflow of transcript → AI → SOP]

**The prompt:** "Here's a training recording transcript. Create a detailed SOP that a new employee could follow step-by-step, including edge cases and decision points mentioned."

### Method 2: The Reverse AI Interview
This is the gold standard when experts are available.

The AI acts as a new employee interviewing the expert. The key: **one question at a time.** Each answer informs the next question. This prevents the expert from forgetting details.

**The prompt:** "Act as a new employee. Interview me to understand how I do [task]. Ask one question at a time—each answer informs your next question. At the end, create a detailed SOP."

30-60 minutes yields absolute gold.

### Method 3: Documentation Audit
Expert unavailable? Mine their digital footprint.

Slack messages, email threads, Notion pages, Google Drive docs, Confluence wikis—consolidate everything related to their expertise. Pass it to AI for synthesis.

Less ideal than interviews, but extracts 60-80% of captured knowledge.

## SEGMENT 3: DISTRIBUTION LEVELS (3:30-5:00)

[APPLICATION]
Once you have the SOP, you need to distribute it. But not all teams are ready for the same level.

**Level 1: Prompt Library** (Low AI Maturity)

A shared folder with proven prompts. Each prompt includes: the prompt itself, a use case study, and a template others can customize.

Highest user involvement—but teaches AI fluency while distributing knowledge.
Start here if your team is new to AI.

**Level 2: Projects & Gems** (Medium AI Maturity)

ChatGPT Projects, Claude Projects, Gemini Gems—preconfigured AI assistants for specific tasks.

Paste your system prompt from the SOP. Upload reference files. Users interact naturally; AI applies expert knowledge automatically.

[VISUAL_CUE: Screenshot of Claude Project setup]

This is the sweet spot for most teams. Expert knowledge embedded in reusable AI tools.

**Level 3: Claude Skills** (High AI Maturity)

Autonomous AI that activates based on context. Unlike Projects where you go to them—Skills COME TO YOU. Triggered automatically in any conversation.

Most automated, least user involvement. Reserve for well-defined, high-frequency tasks.

Coming soon to ChatGPT and Gemini—this is the future of knowledge distribution.

## BONUS (5:00-5:30)

[PAYOFF]
Here's what nobody tells you: An SOP that's 75% complete is USELESS for AI.

Edge cases, nuances, and subtle judgment calls are where experts shine—and where incomplete SOPs fail. The AI needs the full recipe, not just the ingredients.

**The quality test:** Could a new hire follow your SOP without asking a SINGLE question? Does it cover edge cases the expert handles intuitively?

Garbage in, garbage out. Invest in SOP quality now.
