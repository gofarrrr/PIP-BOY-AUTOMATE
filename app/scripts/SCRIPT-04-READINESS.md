## SEGMENT 1: THE ASSESSMENT (0:30-2:00)

[CONCEPT]
Before chasing AI pilots and agent deployments, you need to know where you actually stand. This isn't about aspirations—it's about current reality.

The diagnostic checks 5 things:
1. **Executive mandate** — Is there real commitment with resources?
2. **Grassroots activity** — Is there bottom-up experimentation?
3. **Governance** — Is there clear ownership and policy?
4. **Data accessibility** — Is information consolidated and reachable?
5. **Culture** — Is there appetite for risk and experimentation?

[VISUAL_CUE: Show 5-point diagnostic flow]

Let me walk you through quickly:

**Executive mandate** means more than verbal encouragement. It requires dedicated budget, time explicitly allocated—not "do AI on the side"—and leadership that understands demo ≠ production.

**Grassroots activity** is employees using ChatGPT on their own initiative, teams building demos, informal knowledge sharing. Without coordination, this creates shadow AI—but the energy is valuable.

[DOPAMINE_TRIGGER]
Here's the key insight: Organizations with established AI governance frameworks are 6.6% more agent-ready on average. That may sound small—but it compounds across everything.

## SEGMENT 2: THE 5 ARCHETYPES (2:00-4:00)

[EXAMPLE]

### Archetype 1: The Initiate
**Score: 0-25** | No mandate, no grassroots activity.

This is the true starting line. But here's the flip: Zero prior automation is actually an advantage. No RPA to unlearn. No legacy to rip out. Clean slate.

**Prescription:** Start small. One-page AI policy. Identify natural champions. Pick ONE quick-win use case.

---

### Archetype 2: Grassroots Tinkerers
**Score: 50-60** | Bottom-up experimentation, no top-down support.

You have energy without direction. People are using GPTs on their own—but without mandate, budget, or coordination. Lots of individual usage, few team-wide deployments.

[PATTERN_INTERRUPT]
[VISUAL_CUE: Show "Energy ↔ Direction" matrix]

**Prescription:** Legitimize the energy. Get minimal executive sponsorship. Formalize champions into a task force. Create ONE channel for AI communication.

---

### Archetype 3: Visionary Bottleneck
**Score: 25-50** | Executive excitement, broken foundation.

Leaders push AI without understanding ground-level complexity. Pilots that never reach production. "Just use AI" mandates without time or training. Data trapped in dozens of systems.

**Prescription:** Stop and fix foundations. Pause new pilots—you don't need more experiments, you need plumbing. If governance is missing: establish ownership. If data is fragmented: consolidate first.

---

### Archetype 4: Cautious Incumbent
**Score: 50-70** | All foundations in place, risk-averse culture.

Common in regulated industries. You have mandate, governance, data—but your culture is frozen. Analysis paralysis takes months. Zero or minimal tools approved after 2+ years. Employees afraid to try anything.

**Prescription:** Create a sandbox with guardrails. Define a safe experimentation zone. Start with low-risk internal tools. Shift governance from "blocking" to "enabling with constraints."

---

### Archetype 5: Foundation Builder
**Score: 60-80** | Everything in place, focused on infrastructure over wins.

You have it all—but you're building platforms instead of shipping value. Major data investments underway. Enterprise-grade security. Business units asking "when do WE get AI?"

**Prescription:** Parallel track NOW. Don't wait for perfect infrastructure. Ship something THIS quarter. Internal support bots show ROI fastest.

## SEGMENT 3: THE TARGET STATE (4:00-5:00)

[APPLICATION]

### Agent Ready
**Score: 75-100** | Rare status.

You have executive commitment, governance, accessible data, experimental culture, AND demonstrated wins. Both top-down and bottom-up are connected and moving together.

[VISUAL_CUE: Show all 5 components aligned]

**Signs you're here:**
- Multiple AI tools deployed to production—not just pilots
- Clear governance WITH sandbox for experimentation
- Measured ROI from quick wins
- Employees know what's permitted and use it

**Maintenance mode:**
- Keep iterating—AI evolves fast
- Watch for talent poaching from startups
- Continue celebrating wins to maintain momentum

[DOPAMINE_TRIGGER]
The 2026 theme is Context + ROI. You need both internal context (your data, your processes) AND measurable return. Invest accordingly.
