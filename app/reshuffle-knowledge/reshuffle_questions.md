# Strategic Inquiry: Reshuffle (NotebookLM Prompts)

These questions are designed to extract the specific diagnostic criteria ("signals") needed to build the **Readiness Tab** and identify **Agent Maturity Archetypes**.

## Cluster 1: Diagnosing the Current State (The "View")
*Goal: Determine which of the 4 Value Divisions the user is currently stuck in.*

1. "Based on the book, what are the specific behavioral markers that distinguish a 'Techno-Optimist' organization from a 'Task-Based Misdirection' organization?"
2. "What are the tell-tale signs that a company is falling into the 'Maginot Line' error of optimizing tasks that are about to disappear?"
3. "How does the book describe the transition point between 'supervised automation' (human-in-the-loop) and true 'systemic rebundling'? What are the early indicators?"
4. "Can you list the specific 'blind spots' associated with each of the first three views on value distribution (Luddite, Optimist, Task-Based)?"
5. "What questions should a leader ask themselves to determine if they are viewing AI as a 'tool' versus a 'coordination force'?"

## Cluster 2: Assessing Worker/Agent Positioning
*Goal: Identify if the agent/user is 'Above' or 'Below' the algorithm.*

6. "What are the concrete characteristics of a job role that is 'Below the Algorithm'? Provide a checklist of danger signs."
7. "How does the book define 'Rentable Knowledge' versus 'Sold Labor'? What are examples of 'building blocks' an individual can create to move 'Above the Algorithm'?"
8. "For a 'Nurse Navigator' or 'Sommelier' archetype, what specific 'new constraints' are they solving for? How can we identify similar constraints in other industries?"
9. "What are the psychological or structural barriers that prevent workers from moving from 'Execution' to 'Coordination' roles?"
10. "Does the book provide a framework for auditing a current portfolio of skills to see which constitute 'leverage' and which are 'commodities'?"

## Cluster 3: The 5 Levers of Control (The "How")
*Goal: Determine which coordination levers are available or missing.*

11. "In the context of 'Representation', what does it mean to 'define the map'? Can you give examples of companies that won by controlling the ontology vs. the technology?"
12. "How can a small player or individual leverage the 'Decision' lever without owning the platform? Is it possible?"
13. "What are the specific 'Governance' mechanisms that distinguish a 'Field Reshaper' from a standard platform owner?"
14. "Explain the concept of 'Coordination without Consensus' in technical detail. How does AI specifically enable this, and what are the prerequisites?"
15. "Which of the 5 levers (Representation, Decision, Execution, Composition, Governance) is the most accessible entry point for a new entrant trying to disrupt an incumbent?"

## Cluster 4: Maturity Archetypes (Strategic Postures)
*Goal: Flesh out the rubric for the Readiness Diagnostic.*

16. "Create a comparative table for the 4 Strategic Postures (Reactive Optimizer, Anticipator, Logic Shifter, Field Reshaper) across dimensions of: Risk Tolerance, Investment Focus, and Key Metrics."
17. "What is the 'tipping point' behavior that signals a move from 'Anticipator' (Wayne Gretzky) to 'Logic Shifter' (Steph Curry)?"
18. "How specifically does a 'Field Reshaper' (Tiger Woods) 'change the structure of the playing field'? What are the steps to structural change?"
19. "What are the common failure modes for a 'Reactive Optimizer' trying to adopt AI tools without changing their strategy?"
20. "If we were to score an organization's 'Readiness' for the AI economy, what 5-7 distinct variables should be weighted most heavily based on the book's thesis?"

## Cluster 5: Future-Proofing & Rebundling
*Goal: Actionable steps for the 'Prescription' phase of the diagnostic.*

21. "What is the detailed process for 'identifying the new constraint' in a system? Does the book offer a methodology?"
22. "How should an organization 'rebundle' its workflows to prioritize 'Coordination' over 'Efficiency'?"
23. "What are the 'Artifacts of System Constraints' that are most likely to dissolve next? (e.g., job descriptions, org charts, rigid processes)."
24. "Can you extract a 'Playbook' for the 'Logic Shifter'? What are the first 3 moves they make?"
25. "How does the book suggest we measure 'Return on Coordination' vs 'Return on Investment'?"

## Cluster 6: Deep Dives / Edge Cases
26. "How does the concept of 'Coordination without Consensus' apply to internal team structures versus external market platforms?"
27. "What is the role of 'unstructured data' in shifting power from 'Composition' (APIs) to 'Decision' (AI Models)?"
28. "Does the book discuss the 'timing' of reshuffles? How do we know if a system is ripe for rebundling or still stable?"
29. "What counter-arguments or risks does the author present regarding the 'Field Reshaper' strategy?"
30. "Summarize the 'Tiger Woods' metaphor in detail: How exactly did he change the field, and how does that map to business strategy?"
