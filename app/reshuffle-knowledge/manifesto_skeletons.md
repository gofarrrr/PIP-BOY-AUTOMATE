# The Manifesto – Skeleton Proposals

## Core Themes Extracted

### From Task Chart
- **The Spectrum**: Automate ↔ Augment ↔ DIY (not binary)
- **Judgment as the dividing line** – AI stops where taste, empathy, ethics begin
- **The Operator Imperative** – 95% of AI pilots die from neglect
- **Risk isn't just technical** – organizational resistance kills projects

### From Strategy Chart  
- **Atoms vs Bits** – physical business = tailwind, digital = hurricane
- **The Three Layers** – Production (commoditizing) → Judgment → Execution
- **The Mid-Tier Squeeze** – squeezed by lean startups AND giants
- **The Death Trap** – 20% efficiency gains won't save you
- **Distribution is the new moat** – production is commodity

### From Knowledge Chart
- **Bottleneck = Business Risk** – your best employee is your biggest risk
- **Extract → Package → Distribute** – the knowledge lifecycle
- **Tribal vs AI Gap** – two diseases, similar extraction
- **Prompting Hill** – from Zero-Shot to Agents
- **Vault Synchronization** – meetings update reality, vault must follow

> *Simple, memorable, invites exploration*

```

1. What should you automate?

   Some aren't worth any.
   The chart reveals which is which.

2. Will AI save your business—or end it?
   If you move atoms, it's a tailwind.
   If you sell production, you're in trouble.
   The middle is a death trap.

3. Where is your knowledge trapped?
   Your best employee is your biggest risk.
   When they leave, that knowledge dies.
   Extract it before you lose it.

We built three diagnostic frameworks to answer these.
Click any chart. Start diagnosing.
```

## Skeleton 4: "The Thesis"

> *Intellectual, manifesto-like, big-picture framing*

```
THE THESIS

The AI conversation is broken.

One camp says: "AI will replace everything."
The other says: "AI is just a tool."

Both are wrong.

The truth is nuanced:
- Some tasks should be automated. Most shouldn't.
- Some businesses will thrive. Many will die.
- Some knowledge can be extracted. Some can't.

The question isn't "should we use AI?"
The question is: "Which AI decisions actually matter?"

We built three frameworks to answer that:

TASK → What's worth automating?
STRATEGY → Will AI help or hurt your business?
KNOWLEDGE → How do you extract what's trapped in heads?

No hype. No fear-mongering.
Just structured thinking for structured decisions.

[Start Thinking]
```

## Skeleton 5: "The Warning"

> *Urgent, stakes-focused, risk-first*

```
THE WARNING

MIT: only 5% of AI pilots reach production.
IBM: 75% of AI solutions fail to deliver ROI.
Deloitte: 85% don't see significant measurable value.

The problem isn't the AI.
The problem is the decisions around the AI.

Which tasks to automate?
→ Task Assessment (the first chart)

Which strategy to pursue?
→ Strategy Diagnostic (the second chart)

Where is knowledge trapped?
→ Knowledge Playbook (the third chart)

We didn't build another AI tool.
We built the framework for deciding when to use them.

The stats above don't have to apply to you.
But they will—unless you think before you act.

[Think First]
```

## My Recommendation

**Skeleton 2 ("The Uncomfortable Truths")** or **Skeleton 5 ("The Warning")** 

**Why:**
- Opens with credible statistics (creates trust)
- Challenges the reader (creates engagement)
- Sets up the charts as solutions to real problems
- Matches the "optimistic but honest" brand tone

**Skeleton 3 ("The Journey")** is also strong if you want a gentler, narrative approach.
