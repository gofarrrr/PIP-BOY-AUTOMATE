# Insights: Cluster 2 - Assessing Worker/Agent Positioning

## 1. Below the Algorithm (Danger Signs)
*You are directed by the system, not the director of it.*
- **Algorithmic Allocation**: Work is assigned, not chosen (e.g., "blue line" routing).
- **Standardization**: Strict templates, no room for differentiation/style.
- **Loss of Pricing Power**: Price set by system supply/demand.
- **Metric-Based Governance**: Judged only by measurable metrics (speed/login time); intangible skills (empathy) are invisible.
- **Agency Reversal**: The system optimizes you for *its* efficiency.

## 2. "Rentable Knowledge" vs. "Sold Labor"
*Shift from Labor to Capital.*
- **Sold Labor**: Linear, time-bound, requires presence.
- **Rentable Knowledge**: Unbundled expertise turned into a "building block" (capital). Scalable, recombinable.
- **Examples**:
    - *Likeness/Voice*: Licensing models instead of acting scenes.
    - *Agentic Workflows*: Selling the *system* that produces the outcome, not the consulting hours.
    - *Codified Credentials*: Verified "digital wallet" of skills for auto-matching.

## 3. Rebundling Archetypes (Solving New Constraints)
*Sommelier & Nurse Navigator succeed by solving what tech broke.*
- **Sommelier Archetype**: 
    - *Old Constraint Gone*: Scarcity of Info (Wine info is free).
    - *New Constraint Solved*: **Choice Overload & Emotional Risk** (Confidence/Curation).
- **Nurse Navigator Archetype**:
    - *Old Constraint Gone*: Speed of execution.
    - *New Constraint Solved*: **Coordination Gaps & Contextual Translation** (Coherence across fragmented experts).

*Heuristic*:
- If AI makes generation cheap -> Look for **Verification/Liability**.
- If AI optimizes silos -> Look for **System-Level Coordination**.

## 4. Barriers to Moving Up
- **Invisibility**: High-context troubleshooting is often unpaid/untracked by algos.
- **Execution Bias ("Vibe Coding")**: Prioritizing impressive outputs (the "vibe") over rigorous system logic.
- **Reskilling Fallacy**: learning skills for *tasks* that are vanishing, instead of constraints (risk/judgment).

## 5. Value Audit (Leverage vs. Commodity)
1. **Intrinsic Value**: Meaning/Skill level (High skill != Security).
2. **Economic Value**: Scarcity/Demand. (If AI does it, scarcity drops -> Commodity).
3. **Contextual Value**: **Focus Here.** Resolves a system bottleneck (Risk/Liability/Coordination), even if the task execution is cheap.
