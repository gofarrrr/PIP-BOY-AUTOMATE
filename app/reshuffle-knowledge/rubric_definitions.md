# Readiness Diagnostic Rubric ("Reshuffle" Framework)

This document defines the logic for the **Readiness Tab**. It maps user inputs to one of four "Agent Maturity Archetypes" based on Sangeet Paul Choudary's *Reshuffle*.

## I. The 4 Agent Maturity Archetypes (Strategic Postures)

### 1. The Reactive Optimizer ("The Average Player")
- **Core Logic**: *Efficiency*. Playing the same game faster.
- **Mindset**: "Tool" mindset. "How do I do X task cheaper?"
- **Behavior**: Automating visible tasks without changing workflows.
- **Risk**: "The Maginot Line" — Perfecting an answer to a question that is becoming irrelevant.
- **Diagnostic Signal**: Focus on headcount, speed, and existing silos.

### 2. The Anticipator ("The Wayne Gretzky")
- **Core Logic**: *Positioning*. Moving to where value is heading.
- **Mindset**: "Prediction" mindset. "Where is the puck going?"
- **Behavior**: Investing in data/insight to make better human decisions.
- **Risk**: "The Tool Integration Trap" — Competing on tools anyone can buy.
- **Diagnostic Signal**: Focus on forecasting, data acquisition, and "first mover" advantage.

### 3. The Logic Shifter ("The Steph Curry")
- **Core Logic**: *Rewiring*. Changing the internal physics of value creation.
- **Mindset**: "Engine" mindset. "The AI is the decision maker."
- **Behavior**: Moving decision rights from Human to Machine (e.g., Farmer -> Tractor).
- **Risk**: "Liability Trap" — Owning the risk of the machine's decisions.
- **Diagnostic Signal**: Focus on "Yield/Outcome" rather than speed. Changing the *unit of analysis*.

### 4. The Field Reshaper ("The Tiger Woods")
- **Core Logic**: *Governance*. Redefining the ecosystem boundaries.
- **Mindset**: "System" mindset. "I set the rules others play by."
- **Behavior**: Building coordination layers that force others to adapt (e.g., "Tiger-proofing" the course).
- **Risk**: "Ecosystem Revolt" — Partners leaving due to squeeze.
- **Diagnostic Signal**: Focus on "Coordination" and "Control Points". Solving *other people's* constraints.

## II. Diagnostic Dimensions (The 5 Variables)

The diagnostic tool will score users on these 5 scales to determine their Archetype.

| Variable | Level 1 (Reactive) | Level 2 (Anticipator) | Level 3 (Logic Shifter) | Level 4 (Field Reshaper) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Unit of Analysis** | **Task** (Steps) | **Trend** (Forecasts) | **Outcome** (Yield) | **Constraint** (Bottlenecks) |
| **2. Role of AI** | **Tool** (Assistant) | **Advisor** (oracle) | **Engine** (Performer) | **Environment** (Infrastructure) |
| **3. Knowledge Flow** | **Siloed** (Files) | **Aggregated** (Dashboards) | **Agentic** (Actionable) | **Unified** (Shared Truth) |
| **4. Coordination** | **Meetings** (Tax) | **Standards** (APIs) | **Automated** (Triggers) | **Zero-Tax** (Implicit) |
| **5. Value Strategy** | **Scarcity** (Labor) | **Insight** (Data) | **Decision** (Judgment) | **Control Point** (Governance) |

## III. Diagnostic Questions (Draft)

These questions will drive the interactive "Chat with the Diagnostic" flow.

**Q1 (Unit of Analysis):** "When you think about applying AI, what is your primary goal?"
- A: "Do our current specific tasks faster/cheaper." (**Reactive**)
- B: "Spot changes in the market earlier than competitors." (**Anticipator**)
- C: "Change the way we measure success (e.g., from speed to yield)." (**Logic Shifter**)
- D: "Solve a broader problem that involves players outside our company." (**Field Reshaper**)

**Q2 (Role of AI):** "Who makes the final decision in your core loop?"
- A: "Humans decided, AI just prepped the data." (**Reactive/Anticipator**)
- B: "The AI decides, humans monitor the exceptions." (**Logic Shifter**)
- C: "The system decides, and it coordinates external partners automatically." (**Field Reshaper**)

**Q3 (Connectivity):** "How do your teams stay aligned?"
- A: "Weekly status meetings and email updates." (**Reactive**)
- B: "Shared dashboards and KPIs." (**Anticipator**)
- C: "Agents automatically trigger next steps across tools." (**Logic Shifter**)
- D: "Our platform acts as the source of truth for us and our partners." (**Field Reshaper**)

---

## IV. Prescriptions (The "Treatment")

**For Reactive Optimizer -> Anticipator**:
- *Advice*: Stop optimizing dying tasks. Audit which "bundles" are stable. Start buying data to see trends.

**For Anticipator -> Logic Shifter**:
- *Advice*: The "Tipping Point". Stop asking AI to predict; ask it to *decide*. Move decision rights from edge to center.

**For Logic Shifter -> Field Reshaper**:
- *Advice*: Look outside. Identify a constraint that affects your whole industry (e.g., trust/risk). Build the layer that solves it for everyone.
