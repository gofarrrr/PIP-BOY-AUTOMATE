# Research Notes: Reshuffle by Sangeet Paul Choudary

## Book Core Thesis

**Main Argument:** AI is not just a "smarter brain" but a "better glue" — a coordination technology that restructures entire economic systems.

**Key Metaphor:** The shipping container analogy — a "dumb" technology that rewired global trade by changing coordination, not intelligence.

## The 4 Tensions (Core Framework)

1. **Workers vs Tools** — AI blurs the line between human skill and machine capability
2. **Tool Providers vs Firms** — Who controls AI tools gains power over those dependent on them
3. **Consolidating Businesses vs Disrupted Industries** — AI enables some to dominate, others to be disrupted
4. **Empowered Individuals vs Incumbents** — AI allows small players to coordinate at scale

## Key Concepts

- **"Coordination without consensus"** — AI enables new forms of organization
- **"Below the algorithm"** — Risk of knowledge workers becoming subordinate to AI systems
- **Value Migration** — From managing scarcity (rare expertise) → managing new constraints (risk, coordination)
- **Asking questions as new scarcity** — When answers are abundant, good questions become rare

## Underexplored Angles (for unique articles)

1. **The Container Analogy — Limits and Extensions**
   - Is AI actually like shipping containers? Where does analogy break down?
   
2. **"Below the Algorithm" — Psychological Dimension**
   - Not just economic subordination but identity/agency crisis for knowledge workers
   
3. **The Paradox of Coordination Without Consensus**
   - What happens to democracy/voice when AI coordinates without human agreement?
   
4. **Value Migration — Winners and Losers in Poland/CEE Context**
   - How does this play out in non-Silicon Valley economies?
   
5. **Asking Questions as New Scarcity — Education Implications**
   - Current education optimizes for answering, not questioning
   
6. **Critique of "Econ-Bashing"**
   - Is Choudary strawmanning economics? What do economists actually say about AI?
